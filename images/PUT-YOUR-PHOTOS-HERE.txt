PUT YOUR PHOTOS IN THIS FOLDER
==============================

Save your photos here with these exact names. The website looks for them by name.
All three are optional: if one is missing, the website shows a plain green and gold
plate in its place, so nothing looks broken.

1. portrait.jpg
   A portrait of you. It appears on the Home page profile card and on the About page.
   A clear photo taken in daylight is best. Tall (portrait) shape, at least 900 pixels wide.

2. fieldwork.jpg
   A photo of your work: an interview, a visit, a workshop, a burial site,
   or a record you are allowed to show. It appears in the dark section on the Home page.
   Tall (portrait) shape, at least 1200 pixels wide.

3. hero.jpg
   The wide photo behind the big headline on the Home page. A landscape shot works best:
   family land, a town, a ceremony, or you at work. At least 1600 pixels wide.
   Keep the left side of the photo calm, because the headline sits over it.

To reload after adding a photo: press Ctrl + Shift + R on the page.
