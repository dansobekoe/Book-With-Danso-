The genealogist

Danso Bekoe

Helping families find their roots and understand the journey their families have taken.

I am a professional genealogist and family history consultant, based in Accra. I specialise in West African history: the records that exist, the ones that were never kept, and the stories that survive only in memory.

I also conduct oral history interviews, travelling to meet elders and record their memories before they are lost. If you are reconnecting with your roots, I can guide you on the ground and stay with you through the journey.

How I work

Three rules I hold to

Family history involves people who are still living and memories that are not mine to share. These are the rules I hold to.

Evidence first

Every line in a family account is traced to a source: a document, a recording, or a named elder who gave the account. Where the evidence is thin, the account says so.

The family stays in charge

You decide which branches to research and who may be contacted. Nothing is recorded or shared without the family's agreement.

Records are kept

Interviews are recorded with permission. Photographs are labelled and dated. The finished account belongs to the family.

Much of what matters to a Ghanaian family is not online. It sits with elders, in family land, in the names given to children, and in the way a funeral is arranged.
Danso Bekoe, genealogist
